Suburb Address Count

Drop a suburbs-and-localities shapefile and an addresses shapefile, as two zips or as one zip holding both folders. Every address point is placed inside its suburb polygon and tallied. Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

You can also drop zips anywhere on the page. Layers must not overlap: each address and suburb is counted once per layer it appears in.

Built for LINZ Data Service exports (NZ Addresses and NZ Suburbs and Localities, shapefile format), but any zipped point and polygon shapefiles in the same coordinate system work. Points on a shared boundary are counted once. Large national extracts take tens of seconds.
